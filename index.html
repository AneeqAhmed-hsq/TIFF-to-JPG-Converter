Professional TIFF Format

TIFF (Tagged Image File Format) is used in photography, publishing, and scanning for its high quality and features:

🎨 Lossless compression
📖 Multiple pages
🖼️ Layers & transparency
💾 CMYK color support
✅ Convert to JPG for web

TIFF files preserve maximum quality but are large. JPG provides excellent quality with much smaller file sizes.
